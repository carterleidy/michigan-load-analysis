# michigan-load-analysis
Weather-normalized load analysis for MISO Local Resource Zone 7 (Michigan's Lower Peninsula)

## Note on geography
EIA-930 subregional demand for MISO is reported as `0027` = Zones 2 and 7
combined. Zone 7 covers most of Michigan's Lower Peninsula; Zone 2 covers
most of the Upper Peninsula plus eastern Wisconsin. This series is therefore
Michigan-dominant but not Michigan-only. The southwest corner of Michigan's
Lower Peninsula is in PJM and is excluded entirely.
